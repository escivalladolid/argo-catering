Booking Policy

  • Inquiry submission does not equal booking confirmation.
  • Final booking requires quotation acceptance and required payment verification.
  • Guest count changes may affect the final price.
  • Changes to confirmed bookings require staff approval.

Payment Policy

  • A deposit may be required to confirm your booking.
  • Payment submission does not automatically mean payment verification.
  • Full payment terms will be included in your official quotation.
  • Accepted methods: GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, and Cash / On-site payment.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Cancellation after payment may be subject to the company's cancellation and refund policy.
  • Cancellation after preparation has started may have non-refundable costs.
  • Refund amounts, if any, will be communicated by our staff upon review.
  • Cancellations close to the event date may be handled differently than early cancellations.
